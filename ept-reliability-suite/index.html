The problem

Reliability evidence lives in six places. Decisions need all of it.

Test data sits with validation, field data with service, supplier data with quality, and customer feedback with clinical teams. EPT Reliability Suite puts every source in one project, analyses each with the right method, and carries the findings straight into an 8D.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does my data leave my computer?

No. The analysis runs in your browser, and your projects, studies and reports are stored there or in files you save. DuurveX-Tech does not receive your engineering data.

What do I need to use it?

A current version of Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome or Safari on Windows, macOS or Linux, and a work email address. Nothing is installed.

Who can get access?

Access is by approval only. We review each request and approve named work email addresses; every sign-in uses a one-time code sent to that address.

What happens when the trial ends?

Modules lock until you activate a licence key. Your projects and data are kept, and they open again as soon as the modules are licensed.

Can I buy only the modules I need?

Yes. Buy single modules, the Design, Field or Clinical pack, or the complete suite, for as many named users as you need. Contact us for pricing.

Is the analysis validated?

The methods are standard reliability engineering models, and their implementation was checked against hand calculations. Results are estimates that your engineers should review before making design, safety or release decisions; the user guide explains every method.

Is there an online edition for teams?

An online edition with company sign-in, shared team projects and administrator controls is in development. Ask about joining the pilot in the trial form.
