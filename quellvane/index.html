The problem

A device fails mid-case. It is swapped, thrown away, and forgotten.

Each team sees one small failure. Nobody sees the pattern across rooms and weeks, until a whole lot has reached patients.

How it works

From the morning huddle to a verified fix

Four steps, all in one project. In the room, everything happens by voice.

01

Brief the team before the first case

The team briefing lists lots not to open, lots to use with care, room readiness and fixes in progress. Quellvane reads the warnings aloud in the room.

“Quellvane, today's briefing.”

02

Prep the room by voice

The checklist fills as the team talks and lays out devices. Every lot is checked against the hold list before a package is opened.

  • “Pressures zeroed.” “Defib checked.” Items tick green.
  • A held lot turns red and Quellvane says so out loud.
  • “Room ready, 12 of 12” is recorded with the time.
03

Capture problems hands-free, and catch the lot

“It's kinked, open another one.” Quellvane drafts a report on the device in use, notices the swap, and the team confirms by voice at sign-out. Every report joins its lot.

  • Confidence that a lot has a real problem, not bad luck
  • Patients and days between problems, against normal
  • A person always decides on a hold
04

Close the loop with FRACAS and 8D

Each problem moves from report to containment, root cause, corrective action and verification. D1 to D8 are drafted from the evidence in every study, and an incident closes only when enough clean devices prove the fix worked.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does Quellvane store patient information?

No. It records devices, lots, times, rooms and procedure types only. The agreement every user accepts forbids entering patient information, typed or spoken.

Is it a medical device?

No. Quellvane does not diagnose, treat or support clinical decisions for any patient. It gives teams device reliability evidence; qualified people make every decision.

Does anyone have to touch a screen in the room?

No. The checklist fills by voice, devices are identified as they are laid out, and reports are confirmed by voice at sign-out. Desk screens are for the quality office and biomed.

Where does our data go?

It stays on your devices and in project files you save. DuurveX-Tech does not receive it. Only sign-in details are processed by our access provider, Cloudflare.

How does voice work, and is audio kept?

Voice uses your browser's speech recognition, which may process audio through the browser maker's service. Quellvane keeps only device events and stores no audio. Use voice in procedure rooms after your privacy and IT teams approve it.

What do we need to run it?

A current Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS or a tablet, a work email, and for voice a microphone. It installs from the browser in one click and works offline.

Who can get access?

Clinicians, quality, biomedical engineering, supply and IT teams at healthcare organisations, and engineers who support them. Each request is reviewed, and access is granted per work email.

Are the calculations validated?

The methods are standard reliability engineering models, checked against independent calculations. Results are estimates for your engineers and clinical leaders to review; every module's Methods tab explains them.
